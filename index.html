    How it works and limitations
    • This page launches translation websites. It does not retrieve or display their translations.
    • Automatic source selection is a simple writing-system estimate: Japanese kana, Korean Hangul, Chinese ideographs, or English as the fallback.
    • Mixed-language text and Japanese containing only kanji can be misclassified. Select the source manually when necessary.
    • The Google link requests Traditional Chinese for the Chinese target. DeepL and Baidu links use a general Chinese code.
    • Provider URL formats may change. If text is not prefilled, paste it directly into the provider's website.
    • Opening a provider again requests a new window or tab. Existing windows do not update automatically.
    • Close All is best-effort. Close remaining translator tabs manually, especially before refreshing this page.
    • The 5,000-character limit is this app's limit, not a guarantee that a provider accepts that much text through a URL.
    Privacy: Opening a translator includes your text in its destination URL. Avoid passwords, credentials, and confidential information. This app does not save your text to local storage.
